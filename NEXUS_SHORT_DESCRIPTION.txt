# Option 1 (Recommended - 285 characters):
Redesigns spellcasting into two tactical layers: indefinite, damage-unbreakable Focus buffs (Bless, Haste, Shield of Faith) and native Concentration conduits (Spirit Guardians, Wall of Fire). Maintain both in Overclock with Advantage saving throws! Features War Caster & martial perks.

# Option 2 (Feature-focused - 263 characters):
Splits spell focus into two balanced layers: damage-resilient Focus buffs (Bless, Haste) and active Concentration conduits (Spirit Guardians, Wall of Fire). Maintain both simultaneously in Overclock with Advantage saving throws, War Caster synergy, and custom UI badges.

# Option 3 (Punchy & Direct - 228 characters):
Solves the 5e concentration bottleneck: damage-unbreakable Focus buffs (Bless, Shield of Faith, Haste) + combat conduits (Spirit Guardians, Wall of Fire). Run both in Overclock with independent Advantage saves and War Caster synergy!
