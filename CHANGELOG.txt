Concentration Redux - Changelog

v1.1.5
- False-Positive Concentration & Out-of-Combat Focus Drop Fix:
  - Fixed a critical engine inspection bug where entity.Concentration.SpellId.SourceType evaluates to "None" by default on entities with a concentration component, causing IsCharacterConcentrating to permanently return true for all characters.
  - Because of this false positive, characters with combat passives (like Bladesingers) were continuously flagged with the Combat Channeling state even when not channeling any spell.
  - Casting Blade Ward (or any Focus buff) then mistakenly registered the character as being in "Overclock" (holding both Focus and native Channeling simultaneously). Any subsequent out-of-combat damage tick (stepping on environmental fire surfaces, poison, or area effects) forced an Overclock concentration saving throw, causing Blade Ward to break prematurely.
  - IsCharacterConcentrating now strictly verifies active concentration via Osi.IsConcentrating / Osi.HasActiveStatus(..., "CONCENTRATION") and validates non-empty spell prototypes, ensuring unburdened Focus buffs remain 100% unbreakable to damage.
- Explicit Boosts & Immunity to Third-Party Overrides Across All Focus Spells:
  - Audited all 40+ INNATE_BUFF_* statuses directly against vanilla engine definitions (Shared.pak), guaranteeing explicit, robust Boosts and mechanics across every single Focus spell (Bless, Shield of Faith, Guidance, Resistance, Blade Ward, Heroism, Protection from Evil and Good, Barkskin, Stoneskin, Haste, Enlarge/Reduce, Fly, Expeditious Retreat, Blur, Magic Weapon, Elemental Weapon, Invisibility, Greater Invisibility, Pass Without Trace, Detect Thoughts, Bane, Bestow Curse, Hex, and Hunter's Mark).
  - 100% immune to other mods (such as DnD 2024 / 5.5e or spell overhauls) tampering with base game status inheritance or stripping properties.
  - Added missing HUNTERS_MARK_OWNER to Target_HuntersMark and HEX_OWNER to Target_Hex (including all Target_Hex_Reapply_* sub-spells) so weapon attacks against marked/hexed targets reliably trigger their extra 1d6 damage.
  - Added Item/MainHand equipment fallback checks to Magic Weapon and Elemental Weapon spells (IF(Item())... IF(not Item()):ApplyEquipmentStatus(MainHand...)), ensuring casting on a weapon directly or targeting a character reliably enchants their main-hand weapon.
  - Corrected Target_Bane to apply via SpellSuccess so its -1d4 penalty strictly triggers on a failed Charisma saving throw.
- Null GUID Resilience & Robust Cast Failure Handling:
  - Added IsNullGuid check to properly handle engine-passed null GUID strings ("NULL_00000000-..." and "00000000-..."), ensuring buff recipient fallback triggers reliably.
  - Guarded CastSpellFailed cleanup so only actual innate Focus spell cast failures clear pending focus states, preventing unrelated failed actions from disturbing active buffs.
- Optimized Tracker Sync Heartbeat:
  - Throttled periodic concentration synchronization from every 15 ticks to a clean 2000ms heartbeat to minimize engine overhead.

v1.1.4
- Haste Functor Condition Syntax Fix:
  - Corrected engine condition syntax in Target_Haste from IF(IsSelf()) to IF(Self()).
  - Resolves an issue where casting Haste on oneself or an ally failed to evaluate the condition, preventing CHANNEL_HASTE and native Concentration from applying.
  - Self-cast Haste now cleanly applies indefinite CHANNEL_HASTE and activates Combat Channeling / Channeling badges, while ally-cast Haste applies the 10-turn CHANNEL_HASTE_ALLY buff.
- Bladesinger DnD 2024 Passives Integration:
  - Registered all DnD 2024 Bladesinger passives (Bladesinger_3_Bladesong, Bladesinger_3_TrainingInWarAndSong, Bladesinger_3_Bladesong_ArcaneRecovery, Bladesong_Unlock, Bladesong_Bladework) under COMBAT_PASSIVES.
  - Ensures Bladesingers immediately receive full Combat Channeling permissions through passive detection alongside active Bladesong stance and subclass checks.

v1.1.3
- Dynamic Channeling Status Indicators & Visual Clarity (No Mechanical Changes):
  - Added dedicated dynamic status badges directly to the character portrait/status tray while actively maintaining a Channel spell:
    - Pure Casters (Channeling): Displays the "Channeling" badge (Arcane Acuity radiant purple eye icon) explicitly detailing in-game what actions are permitted (Cantrips, Bonus Action spells, Quickened spells, defensive reactions like Shield, and War Caster opportunity spells) and what breaks concentration (action-costing levelled spells, offensive reactions like Counterspell, and physical weapon attacks).
    - Martial Gishes (Combat Channeling): Displays the "Combat Channeling" badge (Arcane Synergy radiant purple blade icon) detailing the martial channel stance and explicitly clarifying permissions (including physical weapon attacks).
  - Note: This update is strictly a quality-of-life and visual clarity improvement to make active channel rules immediately transparent in-game directly on your character portrait; it does not change underlying mechanics or combat balancing.

v1.1.2
- Linked Container Spells & Sub-Spells Fix (Bestow Curse, Enlarge/Reduce, Hex, Elemental Weapon):
  - Fixed an issue where spells offering multiple sub-spell options (such as Bestow Curse stat penalties, Enlarge vs Reduce, Hex ability curses, and Elemental Weapon elements) failed to appear as prepareable container spells in the spellbook, causing their raw child sub-spells to leak into the spell list and instantly un-prepare when clicked.
  - Restored the required IsLinkedSpellContainer engine flag and container ID associations across Target_BestowCurse, Target_EnlargeReduce, Target_Hex, Target_Hex_Reapply, and Target_ElementalWeapon so they display cleanly as single prepareable spells in the spellbook and open their choice drawers upon casting.
  - Replaced non-standard phantom spell IDs with true vanilla child entries (Target_Enlarge, Target_Reduce, Target_BestowCurse_ExtraDamage, Target_BestowCurse_SkipTurn, Target_BestowCurse_Ability_*) and properly linked 4th-level Bestow Curse upcast entries.
  - Corrected inheritance in Statuses.txt so Bestow Curse innate buffs inherit directly from valid vanilla base curse statuses (CURSE_ATTACK, CURSE_EXTRA_DAMAGE, CURSE_SKIP_TURN, CURSE_ABILITY_*).
  - Updated INNATE_SPELLS across the Lua engine to track all vanilla child spells and upcast levels.
- Counterspell & Failed Cast Focus Icon Cleanup:
  - Fixed an issue where casting a Focus spell that was Counterspelled or interrupted left an orphaned Focus concentration icon (INNATE_CONCENTRATION_CASTER) permanently stuck on the caster's portrait.
  - Spell focus application is now strictly deferred until spell resolution in StatusApplied, and CastSpellFailed events immediately clean up any pending casts and synchronization trackers.
  - Eliminated the circular self-referential check on ACTIVE_CONCENTRATION_TRACKER inside IsCharacterConcentrating, allowing the concentration tracker icon to cleanly disappear whenever concentration ends or fails.
- Expeditious Retreat & Self-Buff Spells (Focus vs Channeling Fix):
  - Fixed an issue where Expeditious Retreat was labelled as a Focus spell in tooltips but acted as a Channeling spell in gameplay due to vanilla engine spell prefix definitions (Shout_ExpeditiousRetreat).
  - Properly mapped Expeditious Retreat, Blur, Pass Without Trace, and Detect Thoughts across INNATE_SPELLS so they function strictly as indefinite, damage-resilient Focus buffs as designed.
- Defensive Reaction Rules & War Caster Clarifications:
  - Personal defensive reactions (Shield, Absorb Elements, Feather Fall) are unconditionally permitted during active Channeling for all characters without breaking concentration.
  - War Caster reaction opportunity spells are permitted during active Channeling for characters with War Caster.
  - Offensive reactions (Counterspell, Hellish Rebuke) consistently demand total focus and break active Channeling as intended.

v1.1.1
- Spell Weaving (Cantrips, Bonus Actions & Quickened Metamagic):
  - All Cantrips (Level 0): Pure casters now have an active combat loop every turn! All cantrips (Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Eldritch Blast, Sacred Flame, Acid Splash, Shocking Grasp, etc.) are recognized as reflexive magic and can be cast freely without breaking an active Channel stance.
  - Native Bonus Action Spells: Spells requiring only a Bonus Action (Misty Step, Healing Word, Mass Healing Word, Spiritual Weapon, Sanctuary) can now be cast without breaking Channel, enabling fluid tactical repositioning and emergency triage.
  - Sorcerer Quickened Spell Metamagic: Quickening a standard-action leveled spell converts it to a bonus action weave, allowing Sorcerers to spend Sorcery Points to cast leveled spells without dropping their active Channel stance.
  - Standard Action Leveled Spells: Casting standard-action leveled spells (Level 1+) or offensive reactions (Counterspell, Hellish Rebuke) continues to demand total mental concentration and breaks active Channel stances.
- In-Game Tooltip Trailers:
  - Updated all 326+ in-game combat Channel spell cards with explicit spell-weaving guidance: "Casting Cantrips, Bonus Action spells, or Quickened spells does not break Channel."

v1.0.5
- Haste Dual Duration & Intelligent Lethargy System:
  - Self-Cast (Indefinite): Operates as an indefinite Channel spell with unlimited duration.
  - Ally-Cast (10 Rounds): Standard tactical duration of 10 rounds (60 seconds) when cast on party members, companions, or summons.
  - Safe Release (No Lethargy): Voluntarily ending Haste by clicking portrait "X", casting non-whitelisted spells or offensive reactions, swapping Channel spells, or letting the 10 rounds expire naturally on an ally safely releases the recipient with zero Lethargy ("Haste Ended (Safe Release)").
  - Involuntary Disruption (Inflicts Lethargy): Breaking your Channel involuntarily via damage, environmental surfaces, or hard crowd control (Hold Person, Sleep, Hypnotic Pattern, Banishment, Stun, Paralyze) inflicts 1 turn of Lethargy ("Lethargic! (Damage/Incapacitated Disruption)").
  - Sorcerer Twinned Haste Support: Twinned Haste grants the caster an indefinite buff while the ally receives the 10-round buff. When the ally's duration expires, the ally releases safely without Lethargy, leaving the caster's Haste intact.
  - In-Game Tooltips: Fully updated spell card and status tooltips reflecting dual-duration and intelligent lethargy rules.
- Gish Reaction Defensives & Melee Cantrips:
  - Combat Channelers and characters with War Caster (enabling build-your-own gish setups on pure Sorcerers or Wizards) can cast personal reaction defensives (Shield, Absorb Elements) and melee spell cantrips (Green-Flame Blade, Booming Blade, Vengeful Blade) without breaking active Channel spells.
  - Offensive counter-magic (Counterspell) and retaliatory damage reactions (Hellish Rebuke) demand full casting focus and continue to break active Channel spells for all casters.
- Gish Combat Stances & Class Forms:
  - Activating martial class stances and disciplines (Bladesong, Symbiotic Entity, Bind Pact Weapon, Hexblade's Curse, Weapon Bond, Monk Ki maneuvers/stances, Bardic Blade Flourishes, and martial Channel Divinities) never breaks an active Channel spell.
- Loss of Control & Hard CC:
  - Hard incapacitation conditions (Stunned, Paralyzed, Sleeping, Incapacitated, Banished) reliably sever both Focus and Channel and trigger full disruption penalties.
